# Backlog

**Started 2026-09-05.** Things found, agreed, or deferred during the build that
are not yet done. Ordered by what would cost the owner most, not by effort.

Anything here is either the owner's explicit decision to defer, or something an
agent found and recorded rather than fixed. Nothing here is speculative
polish — if it is on this list, somebody hit it.

## 1. ~~Hint text is doing three jobs~~ — DONE 2026-09-05 (`b5376ad`)

**Owner, 2026-09-05, on the vendor edit sheet:** *"all this explanation makes
the page really busy. maybe we add an arrow explaining the text and by default
it's closed. Budget has similar logic. or maybe add a small i icon, press it,
brings text as a tooltip."*

**Done, delete-first then disclose.** Hint text went from 20,995 characters to
12,572 across 45 files. Nothing over 180 characters remains, where twenty
passages were and the longest was 435.

`src/components/ui/Reveal.tsx` is the disclosure: a `<details>`, closed by
default, with an `i` icon. `<details>` rather than a tooltip because there is
no hover on a phone and this is used on site; rather than a client component
because these forms are server-rendered on purpose, and a `useState`
disclosure would have made every form in the product a client component to
open a paragraph. Eight passages sit behind it — the ones where a reader could
make a *wrong decision* without them (a tax deferral, a date change that
quietly pushes nine other tasks, a flag that cannot be changed later). The
rest was deleted or moved into code comments.

The vendor sheet's Retire and Void sections are now one section, as the owner
asked: they were always one decision.

**What is NOT done:** the standalone prose blocks were shortened but not
counted; 64 `max-w-prose` blocks remain and some are still two sentences where
one would do. Worth a second look once the screens have been used.

## 3. Every screen has the same browser tab title

Measured: `/`, `/projects`, a quote and `/templates` all read
"Northgate Building Group — Quotes". Only `src/app/layout.tsx` sets a title, so
four tabs open on one job are indistinguishable — and the one saying "Quotes"
is the pipeline.

Fix: page-level `generateMetadata` on the record routes, **record first**,
because browsers truncate from the right and the tenant name is identical in
every tab. `/quotes/[id]` → `QT-2026-0001 — Kensington basement finish`;
`/projects/[id]/schedule` → `Schedule — P-0001`; and so on. Wants one commit
across all record routes rather than one screen at a time.

## 7. ~~Destructive actions with no confirmation~~ — DONE (`4b2437b`)

One `ConfirmDialog` on the existing `Sheet`, used by `voidLine` and the quote
status buttons. Cancel takes the wider slot and the focus, so a mis-tap or a
reflexive second press lands on the safe answer.

**The copy says "voided, not deleted" rather than "cannot be undone"**, because
the second would be false — and a confirmation that overstates the stakes is
how people learn to dismiss confirmations without reading them.

Sending and accepting a quote are confirmed; declining is not, because
`reviseQuote` accepts a declined quote and writes a fresh draft.
`Acceptance.tsx` had already reasoned this out for its own decline button.

## 8. ~~`SheetButton` loses typed work after a server refusal~~ — DONE (`4b2437b`)

`SheetButton` re-baselined its dirty snapshot the instant a submit fired,
before knowing the outcome — so a refusal looked identical to a save, and a
later dismissal discarded what was typed without asking.

`REFUSED_EVENT` is now `SAVED_EVENT`'s sibling, fired by `ActionForm`, and the
rule lives in `dirty-baseline.ts` as pure functions with a test that reproduces
the bug and one that proves the fix.

## 11. Deferred features, with the reason

### Phase 3, in this order

**1. Crew.** The owner, 2026-09-05: *"no i don't have people on payroll but
might in future, so crew is genuine."*

A third kind of person: an employee who is scheduled onto work by name and
never signs in. A `users` row for them is a dormant login that exists for
nothing; a `vendors` row files an employee as a counterparty under a tax regime
that has nothing to do with payroll. `assignments.ts` argues for `users`, and
that argument is right *until* there is a payroll — which is why this is Phase
3 and not never.

The seam is already built: `src/lib/schedule/roster.ts` was written to absorb a
third kind with one added query, and nothing downstream of it branches on the
kind of assignee. The work is a table, a settings screen, and widening the
assignment CHECK from "vendor or user" to `num_nonnulls(vendor_id, user_id,
crew_id) = 1`.

The 72-line `src/db/schema/crew.ts` written by an agent is **deleted rather than
kept**: it was never wired, never migrated, and its columns would be rewritten
against whatever the schema looks like by then. The reasoning above is the part
worth keeping, and it is here.

**2. AP/AR.** Deferred by the owner on 2026-09-05. Creating an invoice from a
job already works; what is missing is recording payment. A `payments` table with
status derived from the sum, never a paid flag — for this business partial
payment is the normal case (a deposit, progress draws, a holdback released
sixty days later), so a boolean would be wrong within a week and would answer
neither "how much is outstanding" nor "since when".

### Also deferred

- **Outbound email** (Phase 2 §2.4) — deferred by the owner in favour of a
  log-an-email action. Trigger for revisiting: evidence he uses the reminder
  list. Sending on his behalf before then is worse than nothing.
- **SharePoint mirror, Plan 4b** — **cut from Phase 1** by the owner on
  2026-09-04. The seam exists (`NotImplementedMirror`), the settings screen
  exists, the sync does not. Nothing depends on it. *Still to record in the
  Phase 1 roadmap doc.*
- **OCR on receipts** — the columns (`source`, `ocr_*`) exist and are unused so
  that adding it later is not a migration against a live table. Engine is
  proven and portable per the reuse spec.
- **PDF receipts render inline** — currently download-only, which is the safe
  answer. Inline would need a sandboxed frame and a tightened CSP.
- **Drag to move a task** — argued against and deferred. It conflicts with
  showing what will move BEFORE it moves, and touch-dragging a bar next to
  other bars at 390px is unreliable. If revisited, the honest version is drag
  on the calendar only, for a task with no dependents.

## 12. Smaller things, recorded so they are not rediscovered

- Reminder rule titles repeat the card they sit on ("Won Basement finish —
  confirm start date" under a card titled *Basement finish*). Right on the
  reminders screen where the project is not in view; wrong on a pipeline card.
  Fix at the template: lead with the verb.
- Days-in-stage could carry a tone threshold. **This no longer needs a number
  from the owner**, and asking for one was the wrong question: the thresholds
  already exist as reminder rules (`quote_sent` + 3 days, `no_activity` + 14),
  maintained at `/settings/reminder-rules`. A second setting meaning the same
  thing would disagree with the first the day one of them changed. The owner's
  own suggestion is the right shape — a pipeline card looks concerned when the
  job has an OPEN reminder against it, which is the same fact the reminders
  screen shows and cannot drift from it.
- The pipeline's kind filter could be one-tap links instead of a select.
- `Quote sent` column could carry the summed value of sent, unexpired quotes,
  labelled "quoted" to distinguish it from contract value.
- Column headings on the board are not sticky at desktop widths (they are below
  `sm`). Matters once a column is long.
- `purchase_order_id` on `assignments` has no foreign key, because
  `purchase_orders` does not exist yet.
- `expenses.project_id` is `NOT NULL`, where the spec left it nullable —
  overhead with no job is currently unsupported rather than half-supported.
- `expense_taxes.is_recoverable` is a boolean, so partial recovery (a
  50%-claimable meal) cannot be expressed.
